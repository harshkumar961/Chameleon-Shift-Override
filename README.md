## 🚀 Key Features

Our protocol is divided into three core pillars, each addressing a fundamental aspect of engagement dominance.

### 🎯 Kinetic Targeting Matrix (KTM)
This module redefines the concept of assistance. The KTM is not about blunt force; it is about subtle, predictive alignment. It calculates the trajectory of opposing paint-projectiles and surface slides, offering a dynamic reticle that anticipates the optimal firing solution. This is the difference between aiming at where an opponent is and aiming at where they are *about* to be.

- **Predictive Trajectory Analysis:** Real-time computation of enemy movement curves.
- **Adaptive Sensitivity Scaling:** Automatically adjusts your look-speed based on target proximity and velocity.
- **Customizable Lead Indicators:** Visual cues that are as discrete or as prominent as you prefer.

### 🧲 Spatial Distortion Engine (SDE)
Our flagship locomotion suite takes the concept of the "fly hack" and elevates it to a legitimate art form. The SDE allows for seamless, glitch-free traversal of the vertical axis. More importantly, it introduces the **Teleportation Leap**—a short-range blink that bypasses physical collision maps, letting you traverse walls and obstacles with the fluidity of a light particle. This is not just about moving; it is about redefining the geometry of the battlefield.

- **Zero-Friction Ascension:** Smooth vertical flight controls with adjustable ascent rates.
- **Phased Teleportation:** A micro-blink system that preserves momentum and orientation.
- **Gravity Well Negation:** An optional toggle that nullifies fall damage and landing lag.

### 🛡️ Aegis Integrity Framework (AIF)
Survivability is the cornerstone of any successful strategy. The AIF provides an unbreachable **God Mode**, rendering your chassis impervious to all forms of standard damage. Furthermore, our **Infinite Paint Reservoir** ensures your primary weapon never runs dry, allowing for constant suppressive fire or artistic map coverage. We go beyond the basics by offering a **Latent Health Regen** module that recovers paint integrity over time, keeping you in the fight longer.

- **Absolute Shielding:** Total immunity to hostile paint splatters.
- **Amperage Overdrive:** Maintenance of a 100% paint capacity at all times.
- **Passive Reintegration:** Slow, passive repair of any residual damage whatsoever.

### 🎛️ Command Line Flags (Advanced)

Power users can launch `protocol_control` with specific flags to pre-load configurations.

- `--preset [name]`: Load a specific user preset on startup.
- `--silent`: Starts the interface in the system tray without the main window.
- `--config `: Points to a custom configuration file location.
- `--lang [locale]`: Overrides the default language on launch.

### 🧭 Troubleshooting Common Glitches

- **"Protocol Handshake Failed"** : This usually indicates the target application is not running or the version is incompatible. Check the version matrix in the `docs` folder.
- **"Teleportation Falls Short"** : Ensure the game's collision map has not been updated to flag specific surfaces as 'non-negotiable-blink'. Toggle the function off and on to reset the cache.
- **"Console Window Floods with Errors"** : This is often a visual artifact of the debugger. Type `set debug_mode false` in the console to silence non-critical notifications.
